UNDERSTAND THE PROBLEM

What Is Screen Addiction?

Screen overuse refers to spending excessive amounts of time using smartphones, tablets, computers, or other digital devices in a way that starts interfering with everyday life.

It can affect focus, sleep, relationships, routines, and the time we spend doing activities away from our screens.

💡
The goal isn't zero screen time.

The goal is healthier screen habits and a better balance between online and offline life.

RECOGNIZE THE SIGNS

How Can Screen Overuse Affect Us?

Spending too much time on screens may affect different parts of our daily lives.

👁️

Eye Strain

Long periods of screen use may contribute to eye discomfort, dryness, and headaches.

💤

Sleep Disruption

Late-night screen use can make it harder to maintain a healthy sleep routine.

🧠

Focus & Productivity

Constant notifications and interruptions can make it harder to stay focused.

🗣️

Real-Life Connection

Too much screen time can take away from meaningful offline interactions.

SIMPLE HABITS

Tips to Build Better Screen Habits

You don't need to change everything at once. Start with one small habit.

⏰

Set Screen Breaks

Take regular breaks during long periods of screen use.

🔕

Control Notifications

Turn off unnecessary notifications that interrupt your attention.

📚

Try Offline Hobbies

Make time for reading, drawing, sports, crafts, or music.

🚶

Take Screen-Free Walks

Give yourself time to move and enjoy your surroundings without a screen.

📱

Move Social Apps

Move social media apps away from your home screen to reduce automatic checking.

🍽️

Keep Meals Screen-Free

Put your phone away during meals and focus on the people around you.
